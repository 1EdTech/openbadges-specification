## Transparency and Independent Interpretation Practices

Credential metadata should support interpretation by parties outside the issuing organization without requiring additional institutional context.

Issuers implementing TrustEd Credentials are encouraged to publish sufficient metadata to allow:

- learners to communicate achievement value clearly;
- employers and institutions to evaluate achievement rigor;
- verification systems to validate claims consistently; and
- receiving systems to process credentials interoperably.

Transparent metadata practices may improve trust, portability, and long-term utility of Open Badges credentials.
